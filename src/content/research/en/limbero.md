Rocky cliffs and lava tubes on the Moon and planets are scientifically valuable but difficult to access.
Limbed climbing robots use grasping mechanisms at their limb tips to traverse extreme environments with uneven terrain and steep slopes.
This research aims to establish autonomous locomotion capabilities for limbed climbing robots to explore diverse lunar and planetary terrain.
